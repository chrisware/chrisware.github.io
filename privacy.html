Privacy policy: rclone backups

"rclone backups" is a personal OAuth client used by a single person (the owner) to upload their own encrypted backup files to their own Google Drive with the drive.file scope, which limits access to files the app itself created.

  • No data is collected, shared, sold or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • The backups are encrypted before upload; Google Drive stores only ciphertext.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
